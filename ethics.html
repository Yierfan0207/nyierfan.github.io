IT Ethics in Technology

Developer Ethics

Developers should think about user privacy, security, and making software that doesn’t cause harm.

Data Science Ethics

People working with data should try to avoid unfair bias and be honest about how data is used.

AI Ethics

AI systems should be fair, easy to understand, and not hurt users or make bad decisions without control.
