IT Ethics in Technology

Developer Ethics

Developers must ensure privacy, security, and responsible software design.

Data Science Ethics

Data scientists must avoid bias, ensure fairness, and be transparent with data use.

AI Ethics

AI systems should be explainable, fair, and not harm users.
