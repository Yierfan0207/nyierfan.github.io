IT Ethics in Technology

Developer Ethics

Developers should care about user privacy, security, and making software that is safe and responsible to use.

Data Science Ethics

People who work with data should try to avoid unfair bias and be honest about how data is collected and used.

AI Ethics

AI systems should be fair, easy to understand, and designed in a way that does not harm users or make unsafe decisions.
